## Non-negotiable brand rules
Brand: AI with Zaian | الذكاء الاصطناعي مع زيان. Never use the RTA logo or suggest RTA endorsement.
Keep palette unchanged: near-black #0A0F14, navy #101821, cyan #1EC7C6, lime #A8FF00 sparingly, gold #D4A43A sparingly, white #FFFFFF. No intrusive glow, clutter, synthetic-looking people or repetitive AI stock imagery. Every slide's cinematic/photographic background MUST illustrate its actual claim or topic. Never present synthetic rail/work scenes as real evidence.
Human-centred writing: natural, useful, precise, without generic hype or clickbait. Arabic-first for UAE audience; English provides meaningful concise supplementary context, not a literal duplicate. Correct RTL/LTR, numbers, Arabic glyph joining, names Ahmed Zaian / أحمد زيان. Human proofread both languages, including technical terminology and British English where relevant.
Carousel default 1080x1350 (4:5); Story/TikTok 1080x1920 (9:16). Reserve platform control safe zones; verify at 360px wide and device if possible. Platform-specific edits, not blind resizing.

## Editorial value test (Gate A)
Before designing, provide a content brief identifying audience segment, platform, goal, key proposition, why it matters, one original actionable takeaway, evidence and uncertainty. Separate source facts, analysis, opinion, estimates and generated content. Challenge weak assumptions and alternative explanations. Derive a distinct thesis, not a rewritten source. Check duplicates against previous posts. Reject empty, derivative or ungrounded content.

## Agent workflow and quality gates
1. Intake: determine audience, language, format, intent, brand limits and claimed facts.
2. Research: check claims, dates, primary-source credibility and copyright/asset licences. Record accessible source URLs, verification date, claims and numeric calculations.
3. Think: explicitly articulate a novel insight, caveat, practical example and reader benefit. Do not invent stats, case studies, benchmarks or quotations.
4. Draft: write concise fluent Arabic and culturally appropriate British English where English is needed. Editorial reviewer checks meaning and factual framing, not only spelling.
5. Gate A: independent predesign editorial/evidence acceptance. Fail blocks design.
6. Create: topic-specific slide visuals, copy, captions, alt text and platform-specific export. Validate typography, geometry, realism and colour contrast.
7. Gate B: independent postdesign QA using rendered outputs (not just prompts): inspect each slide at full resolution and 360px, verify text and numbers by visual inspection against approved copy and evidence. Assess mobile safe areas, RTL/LTR, visual artifacts, citations, licence rights, claim clarity and CTA.
8. Repair: log each finding with severity, owner, location, reproduction steps, fix and retest evidence. Repeat until zero known unresolved defects. After three unsuccessful repair cycles, escalate to a human, do not claim success or loop forever. Any critical defect automatically fails.
9. Gate C: independently inspect final exported assets, slide order, audio captions where present, filenames, descriptions, source integrity and platform formatting; verify nothing changed after approval.
10. Human editorial approval by Ahmed Zaian BEFORE any publish/schedule/upload. Publishing is opt-in and separate from asset approval.
11. After authorised publish, measure performance against objective; analyse saves, shares, watch completion, meaningful comments, audience relevance and corrections, not impressions alone; record learning for next issue.

## Independent reviewer contract
Producer and reviewer should be distinct roles, ideally distinct model contexts and prompts. Reviewer must not merely agree with producer; actively hunt for counterexamples, unsupported certainty, translation artifacts, numerical mistakes, mobile readability failures, false visuals, unlicensed assets and platform mismatch. Review the actual file and actual caption. Reviewer may FAIL with precise actionable defect report. Never self-certify unexecuted checks.

## Severity and release policy
Critical: false or unsafe operational assertion, unapproved RTA mark, unlicensed asset, fabricated data, deceptive synthetic evidence, privacy leak, blocked human approval => immediate FAIL.
Major: mistranslation, grammatical error altering meaning, wrong percentage/units, illegible mobile text, mismatched image, corrupted RTL shaping, misleading headline => FAIL.
Minor: style inconsistency, non-blocking spacing or redundant phrase => fix before release when practicable; never misstate as zero defects.
Target: Zero KNOWN unresolved defects after actual review, not a promise that no error exists. A numeric quality score (e.g., >=95/100) can support prioritisation but cannot override a Critical/Major failure. Not verified is not passed.

## Release package per post
- Content Brief with audience and platform rationale.
- Approved Arabic and English copy.
- Evidence Register: each substantive claim, source URL, retrieval date, method, uncertainty, license.
- Design assets and full-resolution exports for each platform.
- Independent QA report and closed defect register with retest evidence.
- Final package: caption, hashtags where relevant, alt text, source credit and explicit approval status.
- Post-publication performance record, if authorised and available.
All packages have content ID, version, creator, reviewer, timestamp, platform, and state: DRAFT / IN_QA / REWORK / READY_FOR_APPROVAL / APPROVED / PUBLISHED.

### Brand identity lock

### Recognisable visual grammar: ZAÏAN EDITORIAL SIGNAL (working system name)
Every asset is built around FIVE distinctive devices, not a fixed image template:
1. **Insight Frame**: a restrained asymmetrical rectangular frame around one bold insight; one corner is deliberately open, symbolising an idea not yet closed. Consistent placement but compositional variation.
2. **Signal Stroke**: a small two-step lime editorial underline/tick pointing at the ONE important word or comparison; use once per slide at most.
3. **Evidence Edge**: a fine cyan vertical/right-side strip on factual slides that carries a source label, uncertainty note or illustration disclosure. No decorative factoids.
4. **Reasoning Path**: a minimal 2–4-node route from problem -> evidence -> consequence -> decision, replacing decorative glowing lines.
5. **Quiet Space**: 35–55% of an opening cover reserved as visually uncluttered space; oversized Dubai Font gives the emotional hook.
Use a restrained navy-based look with flat editorial shapes, genuine subject material, subtle texture, purposeful off-grid compositions, dramatic crop and authentic object photography. Avoid glowing glass cards, floating chips, fluorescent grids and overdone 3D. Variations must be topic-driven.

### Creative formats to rotate
A. **The Contradiction**: two opposing statements with one revealing visual device.
B. **The Decision**: visual crossroads, trade-off chart, evidence ledger or decision gate.
C. **The Dissection**: magnified detail of an authentic tool, screen, asset or process, with 3–4 annotations.
D. **One Number, Properly Explained**: one verified large statistic with denominator, source and limitations.
E. **Before / After, With Proof**: paired visual comparison, measured and sourced, no invented improvements.
F. **The Field Note**: genuine object/process editorial photography, a concise insight, and an actionable recommendation.
Rotate methods across posts. Within a carousel, build narrative with intentional visual variety and consistent typography. Do not create same cover layout for every post.

### Topic -> background mapping with no people
- AI agency: actual task workflows, permissions, scoped toolbars, reviewed decision diagrams. NOT bots or faces.
- Railway: accurate assets, signals, track, CBTC maps, technical diagrams. Avoid invented railway operations.
- Business: clean physical workspace objects, documents, decision matrices, real charts; no humans.
- Cybersecurity: credible access diagram and validated security indicators; no random locks.
- Productivity: real interface metaphors, calendars, process maps, not repetitive laptops.
Visual should communicate the precise insight at a glance.

### Hook quality and audience value
Research audience need first. Lead with ONE strong original observation, question or tension. The image should be understood at ~2 seconds on a phone; first sentence should add information. Reject clickbait, false urgency, empty exaggeration, jargon overload and generic motivational copy.
Audience segments: UAE professionals, business owners, AI learners, railway/engineering practitioners. Label intended primary audience and platform, select angle and complexity accordingly. Arabic is primary unless a specific post is designated English-first; English is subordinate contextual support, never a full redundant translation squeezed into the same slide.

### Platform strategy
- Instagram carousel: default 1080x1350 (4:5), usually 5–7 slides: tension -> correction -> evidence -> example -> practical decision -> takeaway. Cover must remain legible in feed crops, review 360px.
- Instagram Story: 1080x1920, one major thought per frame, ample protected safe zones for interface overlays, strong interaction prompt when valuable.
- TikTok: vertical 1080x1920 animated diagrams, screen recordings, typography, physical products/objects, motion-led graphics, captions and narration without human visuals; hook first ~2 seconds, no overlong brand intro. Test watch time rather than assuming a universal perfect length.
- No blind resizing between platforms. Recompose and rewrite hook and transitions for each format.

### Arabic and English type engineering
- Prefer Dubai Light, Regular, Medium, Bold variants as available and verified. Use Dubai Bold for headline, Medium for subheading, Regular for body, numeric callouts with appropriate lining and Western/Eastern digits chosen consistently per slide.
- On 1080px-wide cover: headline starting guide 64–100px; supporting text 30–42px. Adjust based on actual Dubai glyph size; not a substitute for 360px review. Maintain generous line spacing and Arabic diacritics only where meaningful.
- Avoid stretched glyphs, wrong kerning, jagged Arabic shaping, mixing direction inside numbers, orphaned particles or low contrast. Render and proofread ACTUAL exported image; text prompt is not proof.

### QA additions
At Gate A: separate originality check and visual story concept with intended 2-second recognition test.
At Gate B: reject generic AI aesthetic, irrelevant skyline/rail imagery, over-glow, font substitute without disclosure, human depiction, factual graphics with unverified numbers, mobile text failures and duplicate recent layouts.
At Gate C: verify embedded/outlined Dubai font actually appears in exported images; inspect each platform export at actual device-like display and within safe areas. Evidence must show dates, source, licence, Arabic and English proofreader verdict, reviewer identity and each defect resolution.
No "zero errors" without a truly independent reviewer and reproducible result. Any missing font or live analytics check is NOT VERIFIED, not PASS.
