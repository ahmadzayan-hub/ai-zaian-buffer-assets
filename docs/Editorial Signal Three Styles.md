# Editorial Signal | Three complementary visual formats
Date: 9 November 2026
Owner: Ahmed Zaian | أحمد زيان
**Status: design pilot, not approved for publishing**

## Permanent DNA
One fixed brand palette: #0A0F14, #101821, #1EC7C6, #A8FF00, #D4A43A, #FFFFFF. Official Dubai Font for both Arabic and English **required for production**, never silently substitute. No images of people, humanoid robots, RTA marks or generic AI glowing stock art. All substantive facts are cited and verified before design. Topic-specific objects and compositions are mandatory, not generic trains, towers or microchips.

## Three formats
**1. Bold Ideas | الأفكار الجريئة.** One thesis, one tension, one editorial metaphor. Make a reader challenge an assumption; include a thoughtful counterpoint in later slides. Avoid bait and unsupported absolutes. Example: "الأداة تنفّذ. فمن يتحمّل القرار؟". Visual signature: large white headline, restrained lime question, asymmetrical opposing panels.

**2. Data Analysis | تحليل البيانات.** Lead with one verified comparison and reveal what the numbers actually mean. Any graph requires source, period, unit, denominator, uncertainty, scales and accurate labels. If no verified data is available, explain how to assess evidence rather than inventing illustrative percentages. Visual signature: disciplined chart grid, cyan lines, gold comparator, lime highlight only for meaningful result.

**3. Tech Explainers | شرح التقنيات.** Explain the mechanism through simple ordered steps, correctly drawn and not invented; show inputs, processing, checks, outputs and real limitations. No humanoid graphics. Visual signature: sparse right-to-left numbered technical diagram, consistent symbols, readable terms and one takeaway.

## Carousel and platform package
For each theme make separate 1080x1350 Instagram Carousel slides, plus platform-specific 1080x1920 Story / TikTok composition when requested, not stretched copies. Arabic-primary Dubai Font. Check mobile 360px and real Instagram crop; improve readability of all secondary lines. Use concise context-bearing English instead of duplicating the headline.

## Quality acceptance and rollout
Run Gate A editorial originality / evidence; Gate B visual actual-file inspection and font validation; Gate C independent reviewer, real mobile/social UI safe area, owner approval. Reject fake numbers, damaged Arabic RTL, small text, and generic AI graphics. Human approval never implies automatic publishing or enabling recurrence. A score cannot override a blocker.

## Pilot evidence
Six actual 1080x1350 PNG assets, two per format, generated locally in chat and accompanied by 360px previews and SHA256 evidence. **The installed font was Noto Sans Arabic, not Dubai Font**. This alone blocks final brand acceptance. Also secondary copy is too small at 360px; must be enlarged or moved into captions before publishing. No live platform performance claims.
